WHY PLACEMENT RADAR

Stop preparing for everything at once.

Placement preparation can become a long list of disconnected tasks — aptitude, coding, technical concepts, projects, resumes and interviews. Placement Radar turns that list into a priority.

01 / DIAGNOSE

Understand your starting point

Tell Placement Radar what career you are targeting and answer a short assessment designed around that target.

02 / PRIORITIZE

Find your biggest gap

Instead of treating every weakness equally, the radar highlights the area that currently needs the most attention.

03 / ACT

Know what to do next

Get a focused action plan so your next preparation session has a clear purpose.

BUILT AROUND YOUR CAREER GOAL

Different careers need different readiness signals.

A cybersecurity candidate should not be assessed like a business-management candidate. Your target role influences the questions, categories and preparation priorities shown by Placement Radar.

🛡 CybersecurityRole-specific

Security fundamentals, networking, web security, SOC concepts, incident response and security interviews.

💻 Software DevelopmentRole-specific

Programming, DSA, databases, web concepts, Git, problem solving and coding interviews.

📊 Data AnalyticsRole-specific

Statistics, SQL, data analysis, visualization, business analysis and analytical communication.

📈 Business / ManagementRole-specific

Business basics, marketing, finance, strategy, operations, leadership and case interviews.
